<p class="eyebrow">About</p>
<h1 class="mt-1">Hey!</h1>

<p>My name is Tommy Bernaciak. I'm a software engineer based in Łódź, Poland, currently working as a Main Frontend Architect at BinarApps and a Senior Software Engineer at Onwelo.</p>

<p>I've been building software professionally for more than a decade. I started out as a Ruby on Rails developer, moved into AngularJS, spent a couple of years in London building full-stack products at Stratajet, and for the last several years I've focused on React and TypeScript. These days my toolbox also reaches into Next.js, Nest.js, Node.js.</p>

<p>I'm a highly motivated and curious person with a can-do attitude, always learning and hungry for new knowledge. I enjoy my work, I like to do a good job, write clean code, and build applications I can be proud of. I gravitate toward challenging, complicated projects that push me to gain new skills and broaden my horizons.</p>

<h2>What I do now</h2>

<p>At BinarApps I'm responsible for the architecture and development of React applications connected to our clients' APIs - planning and implementing new features that address real needs. Along the way I led one of our React teams as a JavaScript Team Leader, representing the group to management, giving regular career feedback, running technical interviews, and, most importantly, helping everyone on the team grow.</p>

<p>I also spent time as a lecturer at Wrocławska Akademia Biznesu, teaching an Introduction to Software Engineering course through lectures and labs. Sharing what I know is something I genuinely enjoy, which is part of why this blog exists.</p>
